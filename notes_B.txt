2. CEO REPORT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Richard presented the CEO report covering Q4 2025 and the
outlook for FY2026/27.

Key highlights:
  • Revenue for FY2025/26: £127.4m (budget: £122.0m, +4.4%)
  • EBITDA: £19.1m (15.0% margin, up from 13.8% prior year)
  • Net new customers: 342 (churn: 4.1%, down from 5.3%)
  • Employee headcount: 814 (net +47 in FY)
  • Cash position at year-end: £24.8m (no debt drawn)

Richard noted that the US market entry (launched Sep 2025) is
tracking ahead of the investment case, with $2.1m ARR already
booked and a healthy pipeline of $4.8m.

Baroness Whitmore asked about competitive threats from AI-
native entrants. Richard acknowledged the risk but noted that
the company's domain expertise and regulated-industry client
base provide a defensible moat. The product roadmap includes
AI co-pilot features for Q3 2026.

The Board NOTED the CEO report.

3. STRATEGIC REVIEW — ACQUISITION TARGET "PROJECT ATLAS"
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CONFIDENTIAL — This section is restricted to Board members.

Laura Mitchell (McKinsey) presented the due diligence summary
for the potential acquisition of [TARGET REDACTED], codenamed
"Project Atlas."

Key findings:
  • Target revenue: €34m (growing 22% YoY)
  • EBITDA margin: 8% (below sector avg; improvement plan
    identified)
  • Indicative valuation: €110–130m (3.2–3.8x revenue)
  • Strategic rationale: Accelerates European expansion by
    18 months; adds German and Nordics client base
  • Key risk: €4.2m pension deficit on defined benefit scheme

Extensive discussion followed. Prof. Chen raised concerns
about integration complexity and cultural alignment. Kwame
confirmed operational due diligence is ongoing with findings
expected by mid-April.

Anya presented the financing options: (a) all-cash from
reserves + £60m RCF drawdown, (b) cash + equity raise,
(c) earn-out structure reducing upfront consideration.

The Board RESOLVED to proceed to the next phase of due
diligence and authorised management to engage Freshfields
as legal advisors. Final approval will require a further
Board resolution before any binding offer is made.
