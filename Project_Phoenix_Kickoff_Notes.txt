───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. BACKGROUND & OBJECTIVES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Sarah opened the meeting with an overview of the Phoenix
programme. The core objective is to migrate the legacy
customer-management platform (Heron v3.2) to a modern,
cloud-native architecture by Q4 2026.

Key drivers:
  • Heron v3.2 reaches end-of-vendor-support in Jan 2027
  • Current system cannot scale beyond ~80k concurrent users
  • GDPR data-residency requirements not met for EU customers
  • Annual maintenance cost has risen 34% YoY

Target state: containerised microservices on AWS EKS, with
PostgreSQL (RDS) replacing the existing Oracle estate.
